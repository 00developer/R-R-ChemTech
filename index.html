We primarily quote and execute shipments under CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight) and FOB (Free on Board) terms from major Indian loading seaports including JNPT (Nhava Sheva), Mundra, Kandla, and Chennai. We also support CFR (Cost & Freight) or customized container staging upon buyer request.

Every consignment undergoes mandatory pre-dispatch testing. We provide official third-party inspection certificates from SGS, Bureau Veritas (BV), or Intertek verifying grain length, broken percentage, purity, moisture levels, and weight. All shipments include official APEDA, FSSAI, Phytosanitary, Certificate of Origin, and Fumigation certificates.

For verified international importers, government tenders, and institutional trade desks, our standard payment terms are:
• 100% Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C at Sight) opened through a prime top-tier international bank.
• Telegraphic Transfer (T/T): 30% advance deposit with order confirmation, and the 70% balance against clean onboard Bill of Lading (B/L) scan copies.

Our standard MOQ for dry grains (Rice and Wheat) is 1x20ft FCL (Full Container Load), which accommodates 25 to 26 Metric Tonnes. For Fresh Red Onions, the MOQ is 1x40ft High-Cube Reefer Container (~29 Metric Tonnes) equipped with continuous temperature and humidity data-logging. We routinely manage recurring multi-container contracts ranging from 500 MT to 5,000+ MT monthly.

Yes, absolutely. We provide OEM private-label packaging customized to buyer branding. Options include BOPP printed bags, laminated woven Polypropylene (PP) bags, traditional food-grade Jute sacks, and non-woven breathable bags in 5kg, 10kg, 20kg, 25kg, and 50kg sizes with your corporate artwork, barcode, and multilingual labeling (Arabic, French, Spanish, English).

Upon receipt of L/C or deposit, standard containerized cargo is graded, packed, palletized, customs cleared, and loaded onboard within 10 to 14 business days. For urgent spot consignments, our pre-allocated port warehouse inventories at Mundra and JNPT allow vessel loading in as little as 7 days.
