## 🌾 Core Export Commodities

* **Rice Portfolio:** 1121 Basmati (Raw & Sella), Golden Sella, Sugandha, IR64 Non-Basmati Parboiled, Broken Rice.
* **Wheat Portfolio:** Milling Grade Wheat, Durum Grade A Wheat (Bulk & 50kg Bags).
* **Fresh Produce:** Nashik Red & Pink Onions (Reefer Container dispatch, 45mm – 60mm+ grading).

* **Middle East & GCC:** Jebel Ali, Dammam, Jeddah, Sohar, Hamad, Shuwaikh (4–7 Days transit).

## ✨ Features & Architecture

* **Performance & Clean Code:** Built purely with semantic HTML5, Vanilla CSS3, and modern ES6+ JavaScript — zero external framework overhead.
* **Design System:** Bespoke Deep Emerald (`#022C22`) and Royal Gold (`#D4AF37`) luxury industrial theme with fluid micro-interactions and smooth scroll reveals.
* **Interactive Export Desk & Fast-Track Modal:** Instant RFQ modal with auto-selected commodity pills and conversion tracking.
* **Floating WhatsApp Integration:** Persistent floating contact button linked directly to the trade desk (`+91 9065413960`).
* **SEO & Compliance:** Schema.org JSON-LD structured data for B2B search discovery, APEDA, FSSAI, and SGS inspection compliance integration.
